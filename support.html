Support — Verbum Study

Verbum Study is a paid Bible reading and study app for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. There is no account to create and no in-app purchase.

For app questions, bugs, refunds, or privacy questions, email aidenpecyna@icloud.com.

Personal notes, highlights, and reading progress stay on your device. If you are signed into iCloud, they sync through your private iCloud database. Creditch does not run a Verbum server.
